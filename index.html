Robotics and Control · PRACTICE Lab · Advised by Prof. Anushri Dixit (Scholar)

Safe autonomy from spacecraft to learning-enabled robots.

I am a PhD student in Aerospace Engineering at UCLA, advised by Prof. Anushri Dixit in the PRACTICE Lab. My research focuses on safe and reliable autonomous systems at the intersection of reinforcement learning, motion planning, optimal control, and multi-agent decision making.

Before UCLA, I worked as a Guidance, Navigation, and Control scientist at the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO), where I contributed to spacecraft missions including Chandrayaan-3 and Gaganyaan.
